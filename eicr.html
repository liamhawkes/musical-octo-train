Who needs one

When you need one.

  • Landlords — at least every 5 years in England
  • Buying or selling a home
  • Homeowners — recommended every 10 years
  • Before a renovation or extension
  • After a flood, fire or other damage
  • Businesses — for insurers and health and safety

What do the codes mean?

C1 means danger is present and is made safe straight away. C2 is potentially dangerous and needs urgent work. C3 is an improvement we recommend. FI means further investigation is needed. Any C1, C2 or FI makes the report unsatisfactory.

What do landlords need to do?

In England, private landlords must have the electrics inspected at least every five years, give tenants a copy of the report, and complete any required work within 28 days, or sooner if the report says so.

Will the power be off?

Circuits are switched off one at a time during testing, so expect short interruptions rather than a whole day without power.
