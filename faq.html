Certificates and regulations

What is Part P, and does it apply to my job?

Part P of the Building Regulations covers electrical safety in homes in England. Some work, such as adding a new circuit or replacing a consumer unit, must be notified to Building Control. As registered electricians we notify it for you, and you receive a Building Regulations compliance certificate.

What certificate will I get?

It depends on the job. New circuits and larger work come with an Electrical Installation Certificate. Smaller additions, such as an extra socket on an existing circuit, come with a Minor Electrical Installation Works Certificate. Inspections come with an Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR).

How often should my electrics be checked?

Private landlords in England must have an EICR at least every five years. For homeowners, a check every ten years is recommended, and when you buy a home.
