Signs you need one

Is your fuse box due an upgrade?

  • Fuse wire or a wooden-backed fuse box
  • No RCD protection (no test button)
  • A plastic consumer unit
  • Fuses blowing or switches tripping often
  • You are adding an EV charger, heat pump or extension
  • An EICR has recommended an upgrade

Questions

Common questions

Why does it have to be metal?

The wiring regulations (BS 7671) require consumer units in homes to be made of non-combustible material, which in practice means metal.

Will my power be off?

Yes, for part of the day while the new unit is connected. [Confirm typical hours without power]

What if you find other faults?

Adding RCD protection can show up faults that were already on a circuit. If we find any, we will explain them and quote before doing any extra work.
