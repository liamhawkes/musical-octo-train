Accessibility statement

Our commitment to making this website usable for everyone.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Text and buttons have strong colour contrast.
  • The site works with a keyboard, and there is a "Skip to content" link.
  • Pages use clear headings, labelled form fields and descriptive links.
  • Illustrations have text descriptions, and decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • Pages adapt to small screens and respect reduced-motion settings.

Known limitations

The contact form is delivered by a third-party service (FormSubmit), which may show a short verification step after you press send. If you have any difficulty with it, please call or email us instead.

Feedback and contact

If you find any part of this website hard to use, or need information in a different format, please tell us. Call 01525 887 128 or email info@hawkeselectrical.com.
