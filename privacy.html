Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are

Hawkes Electrical Services Ltd ("we", "us") is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can contact us at 17 Home Farm Way, Westoning, Bedfordshire, MK45 5LL, by email at info@hawkeselectrical.com or by phone on 01525 887 128.

Company number: [company number]. ICO registration number: [ICO number, if registered].

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form, email or call us, we collect the details you give us. This is usually your name, phone number, email address, postcode and a description of the work you need.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, arrange a visit, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, carry out and certify the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

If you send us a CV through our careers page, we use it only to consider you for current or future roles.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use a small number of service providers to run this website:

  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, for security.

Where work requires it, we may share details with Building Control, our certification body (NICEIC) or the electricity network operator, for example when notifying an installation.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries that do not lead to work for [up to 12 months]. For customers, we keep job records and certificates for [6 years] after the work, or longer where regulations require it. CVs are kept for [6 months] unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to ask us to transfer it. To make a request, contact us using the details above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
